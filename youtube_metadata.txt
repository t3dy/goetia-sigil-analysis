TITLE: The 72 Seals of the Goetia - Animated Analysis

DESCRIPTION:
----------------------------------------
An animated visualization of all 72 Goetic sigils from the Lesser Key of Solomon,
analyzed using computer vision, graph theory, and statistical methods.

Each seal is animated with psychedelic color effects and distortions,
revealing the hidden structure of these centuries-old mystical symbols.

8 distinct sigil families were discovered through hierarchical clustering.
No correlation was found between a demon's rank and its sigil's complexity.
